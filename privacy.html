Pixle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Pixle ("Developer") provides the mobile game 'Brick Bubble Shooter' ("the App") and takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

  • Advertising ID: used to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance through Google AdMob.
  • In-app purchase information: processed through the Google Play billing system. The Developer does not directly collect your payment details (such as card information).
  • Device and ad data: the ad network (Google AdMob) automatically collects device identifiers, approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions such as ad impressions and clicks, crash logs, and diagnostic information. This data is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and analyze crashes and performance.
  • Google Play Games information: to play online, you sign in with Google Play Games. The App receives your Play Games player ID and display name, and submits your score to the leaderboard. Your score and display name are visible to other players on the leaderboard. This is handled by Google Play Games Services.
  • Data stored on your device: game progress, settings, and a score waiting to be submitted are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the Developer.

Other than the Play Games player ID and display name provided by Google Play Games, the App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your email address. If your device has no internet connection, the App can be played without signing in.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Serving and personalizing ads
  • Processing in-app purchases (the "Remove Ads" product) and verifying purchase history
  • Displaying leaderboard rankings
  • Preventing ad fraud, analyzing crashes and performance, and keeping the service running

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob, Google Play billing (in-app purchases), and Google Play Games Services. Google's Privacy Policy also applies to these services. You can read Google's Privacy Policy at the link below.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 14.

5. Data Deletion and Ad Settings

The App can be used without creating an account. Data stored on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App or through your device settings under 'Apps > Storage > Clear data'. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings under 'Privacy > Ads'. The Developer does not store your personal information on its own servers, so there is no retention period to manage on our side; data held by Google is retained under Google's policies. For details, please see the data deletion guide.

6. Contact

Privacy contact: Pixle (Developer), pixlesoft2026@gmail.com

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in response to changes in laws or the service. Any changes will be announced on this page.
