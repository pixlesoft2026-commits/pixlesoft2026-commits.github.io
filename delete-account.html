Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and personal data yourself, directly in the app on your device. No email or web form is required.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (or My Page / Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message, then tap Confirm to delete your account.
This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be restored.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account information (e.g., email, profile)Deleted immediately
App data and settings stored on your deviceDeleted from your device
Content and activity linked to your accountDeleted immediately
Backup copies and logsRemoved within 30 days

Data we may retain

We may keep limited information only where required by law, for example for fraud prevention, security, or tax and accounting records. Retained data is kept only for as long as legally necessary and is not used for any other purpose.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from your device, but it does not delete your account. To fully delete your account, please follow the steps above before uninstalling.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or run into any problem deleting your account, contact us at pixlesoft2026@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, and we will process your request.
